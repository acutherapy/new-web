## The Benefits of Acupuncture for Work Injuries

Acupuncture is recognized by the State of Hawaii Workers' Compensation system as an effective treatment for pain relief and rehabilitation.

*   **Pain Management without Opioids:** We provide a natural, drug-free alternative to manage chronic pain, allowing you to stay alert and active.
*   **Faster Recovery Times:** By improving blood circulation to the injured area, acupuncture speeds up tissue repair.
*   **Documentation Support:** Our team provides detailed progress reports required by claims adjusters and case managers to ensure your treatment continues uninterrupted.
