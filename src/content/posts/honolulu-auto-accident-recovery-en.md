## Why Choose Acupuncture for Auto Injuries?

After a car accident, your body may go into a state of shock and inflammation. Traditional medical treatments often focus on masking the pain with medication. Acupuncture, however, addresses the root cause:

1.  **Reduces Inflammation:** Acupuncture points trigger the body's natural anti-inflammatory response, accelerating the healing of soft tissue damage.
2.  **Relieves Whiplash:** Whiplash can cause stiffness and restricted motion for months. Acupuncture helps relax deep muscle tension that massage alone cannot reach.
3.  **Calms the Nervous System:** Accidents are stressful. Acupuncture helps regulate the "fight or flight" response, reducing anxiety and promoting better sleep during your recovery.
